Software

I develop research software in R and maintain reproducible computational archives for much of my empirical and theoretical work. The projects below include reusable packages, numerical models, data-processing tools, and complete analysis repositories associated with published research.

R packages

  • sensorsync — R tools for integrating and synchronizing accelerometer and GPS records, developed in connection with work on measuring pedestrian travel from combined sensor data.

  • longformer — tools for managing longitudinal relational research databases in R; used to manage the Tsimane Health and Life History Project research database.

  • kaya — tools for parsing, validating, transforming, analyzing, and visualizing Go game records stored in Smart Game Format (SGF).

  • yamltools — tools for working with YAML-structured plaintext data in R, developed for structured interview and research-data workflows.

  • yamlform — tools for converting a plaintext interview format into XLSForm documents for ODK-based field-data collection.

Reproducible research

For many projects, I maintain public repositories containing the data-processing code, statistical models, simulations, and figure-generation scripts needed to reproduce the published analyses.

Comparative studies

  • corvid-mimicry — data, R code, and Stan models for Vocal mimicry in corvids.

  • wealth-transmission — analysis materials for the cross-cultural study Intergenerational wealth transmission and the dynamics of inequality in small-scale societies.

  • wealth-transmission-son-bias — analysis materials for Differences between sons and daughters in the intergenerational transmission of wealth.

  • polygyny-puzzle — R and Stan code, source data, and reproducible analysis for Greater wealth inequality, less polygyny: rethinking the polygyny threshold model.

  • moralizing-gods-reanalysis — an extended reproducible reanalysis of the historical database and statistical models used to study the relationship between moralizing gods and social complexity.

Empirical studies

  • wealth-mortality-pimbwe — analysis data and code for Understanding the nature of wealth and its effects on human fitness.

  • go-firstmove — analysis code for Strategic social learning and the population dynamics of human behavior: the game of Go.

  • go-learning-eras — data and analysis code for Opening strategies in the Game of Go from feudalism to superhuman AI.

  • tsimane-apoe-reproduction — R and Stan analysis code for research on APOE-ε4 and fertility in a natural-fertility Tsimane population.

  • tsimane-growth-references — data and analysis code for constructing growth references and modeling longitudinal growth among Tsimane forager-horticulturalists.

  • tsimane-afib — analysis materials for the study of atrial fibrillation prevalence and incidence among Tsimane forager-farmers.

Agent-based and numerical simulations

  • SIMani — agent-based simulation of demographically realistic human populations

  • snoobs — simulation and analysis code for Evolutionary decomposition and the mechanisms of cultural change.

  • mosuo-ppr — analysis code for modeling parity progression and offspring-sex preferences among patrilineal and matrilineal Mosuo.

  • causality-variance-fst — numerical models, analysis code, and figure-generation scripts for Why cultural distance can promote – or impede – group-beneficial outcomes.

Research with students and collaborators

  • Cross-cousin marriage among Tsimane — OSF archive for Cross-cousin marriage among Tsimane forager-horticulturalists during demographic transition and market integration.

  • MSrepo_harvest_patch_choice — R and Stan code for simulating and analyzing Inuit foraging patch choice and harvest success.

  • kolam_signaling — data, statistical models, simulations, and analysis code for Entropy trade-offs in artistic design: A case study of Tamil kolam.

  • kolam_coordination — data and analysis code for Limited scope for group coordination for stylistic variations in kolam art.

  • reproducibility-analysis — data, R code, and Stan models for Estimating the reproducibility of social learning research published between 1955 and 2018.

  • Dutch-historical-mobility — R and Stan analysis scripts for a longitudinal study of approximately 300,000 residential moves in the Netherlands between 1850 and 1950.

  • Does literature evolve one funeral at a time? — OSF archive containing the research materials associated with the study of generational turnover and literary change.

  • Tsimane-neighbor-IGEs — reproducible analysis materials for Indirect genetic effects among neighbors promote cooperation and accelerate adaptation in a small-scale human society.
